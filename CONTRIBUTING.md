# Corrections and contributions

Precise corrections, missing hypotheses, and earlier references are welcome.

For an existing finding, [open a correction or prior-art report](https://github.com/LmeowLaunchpad/Legendary/issues/new?template=correction.yml). Include:

- The finding ID and the exact claim or proof step.
- The proposed correction or earlier result.
- A primary reference, theorem number, counterexample, or reproducible check.

Please distinguish an objection to an upstream premise from an objection to the deduction made from it. Both matter, but they affect the record differently.

For a new note, follow the [research standards](docs/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md) and [finding template](docs/FINDING_TEMPLATE.md). Keep speculative consequences separate from proved statements. Disclose AI assistance and the actual scope of any verification.

Do not describe agent agreement as external peer review, or a repository's formalization claim as a locally reproduced proof without a recorded successful check.
